RUNNING AND TRAINING

Build your own pace.

Running better does not always mean running more. It means understanding your starting point, giving your training direction and building consistency without neglecting recovery and sustainability.

From your goal to a running plan.

Before planning the work, I clarify the goal, experience, available time and preferred format. Training is adapted to the starting point and response over time.

Realistic goals

Set a goal that fits your experience, available time and personal priorities. Goals can evolve as new information becomes available.

Progression and recovery

Organise training load gradually and make room for recovery. The plan should respond to the individual, not be followed at all costs.

A sustainable pathway

Reviewing consistency, perceived effort and progress helps adapt the plan. Coaching focuses on running plans, not diagnosing or treating injuries; if pain or clinical concerns arise, consult a healthcare professional.
