RUNNING AND TRAINING

Build your own pace.

Running training is not about accumulating miles without direction. It starts with your current level, a clear goal and a progressive plan that takes account of available time, recovery and consistency.

A plan built around your starting point

Before planning begins, we clarify your experience, goal, habits, commitments and preferred way to check in. This helps shape a realistic pathway that can be reviewed in response to training and changing circumstances.

Goals that fit

A useful goal should be meaningful while remaining compatible with your starting point and available time. Goals can be reviewed as priorities, circumstances or progress change.

Progression and recovery

Training load should build gradually, with recovery treated as part of the plan. The programme should support consistency, not become a rule to follow at all costs.

Review and adapt

Consistency, perceived effort and the overall training process provide useful signals when considering adjustments. Coaching covers running-training planning; it does not include diagnosis or injury treatment. If pain or clinical concerns arise, consult a healthcare professional.
