Design a learning pathway, not just a lesson

Planning begins with the request: audience, starting level, time, context and expected outcomes. These guide the choice of topics, sequence, activities and ways to review progress, keeping the pathway proportionate to actual needs.

Start with the learners

Understanding participants’ experience, needs and prerequisites helps determine language, depth and pace. A useful programme does not assume everyone starts at the same level.

Connect content with practice

Ideas are introduced in a clear sequence and, where possible, linked to examples, cases and exercises. Practice helps turn information into tools people can use.

Check what has been understood

Questions, activities and feedback help show which goals have been met and which topics need further attention. Review is not a formality; it informs any adjustments.
