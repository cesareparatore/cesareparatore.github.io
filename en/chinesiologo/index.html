Movement starts with the person.

Everyone brings a different history, habits and needs. A useful movement pathway starts with listening and realistic goals, respecting individual abilities and circumstances.

Understand the starting point

Build consistency

Activities and progression are planned gradually and adjusted according to the person’s response and what is sustainable over time.

Move with awareness

The pathway focuses on physical activity and general wellbeing; it does not provide medical diagnosis or treatment. If pain, illness or clinical needs are involved, consult an appropriately qualified healthcare professional.
