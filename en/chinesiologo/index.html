MOVEMENT AND PHYSICAL ACTIVITY

Movement starts with the person.

A movement programme should not begin with a standard template; it should begin with the person taking part. Habits, experience, goals, available time and individual circumstances all help shape realistic, sustainable activity.

Give the pathway a clear direction

The first conversation helps clarify the request and understand the starting point. Goals, activities, progression and ways to review the work are then agreed in context. The pathway can be adapted over time in response to the individual, without forcing milestones or offering one-size-fits-all solutions.

Understand the starting point

Learning about habits, movement experience, preferences and goals helps make the work more relevant. If needs arise that call for clinical assessment or healthcare, the appropriate qualified healthcare professional should be involved.

Build consistency

Progression should be understandable and compatible with everyday life. Activities and workload are organised gradually, taking account of the individual response and allowing room for recovery and regular practice.

Respect professional boundaries

The activity described here concerns movement and general wellbeing; it does not include medical diagnosis, prescriptions or healthcare treatment. If pain, illness, injury or clinical concerns are present, seek advice from a doctor or appropriately qualified healthcare professional.
