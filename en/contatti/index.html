THE FIRST STEP

Let’s start with your request.

You do not need to have a fully formed project before getting in touch. Briefly explain the situation, what you would like to achieve and which area you need help with. The first exchange is a chance to understand the request, check whether it fits my scope and identify a realistic next step.

An initial conversation, with no obligation

Once the goal is clear, we can discuss possible activities, boundaries, format and availability. If the request falls outside my expertise or the appropriate service, it is better to say so early and identify a more suitable point of contact.

Message me on WhatsApp

You can use WhatsApp to introduce your request briefly and arrange an initial conversation. Your message only needs the area of interest, your goal and essential context. Please do not send documents, sensitive data or unnecessary health information.

Write by email

Email is the alternative channel for questions, project details and requests that are easier to describe in writing. Write to info@cesareparatore.it.

What to include in your first message

To help me understand your request, mention the area (sport management, kinesiology, running coaching, education or web design), your goal, your location if an in-person meeting is needed and your preferred format. There is no need to share confidential information.
