THE JOURNEY

Nothing is disconnected.

Understand what is complex. Make what matters clear. Build what is needed.

Partinico, ItalyIn person and online, depending on the service

It all started with a screwdriver

I have always been interested in what happens behind things: how they work, what connects them and how they can be improved.

01 — The first computer

In the late 1990s, my bedroom held a 56k modem, a CRT monitor and my first computer. I was fourteen. I wanted to understand what was inside, so I opened it, took it apart and put it back together. At nineteen, that curiosity became my first job in IT.

02 — Discovering movement

Later, my curiosity moved from technology to the human body and sport. Running taught me effort, discipline and consistency. Football brought a different challenge: I founded and managed my own team, learning to lead a group, share goals and organise a project.

03 — From practice to understanding

Returning to running, I experienced sport both as an athlete and as a coach. I wanted to understand movement beyond intuition and experience, so I studied Sport and Exercise Sciences. Study gave me a scientific framework to interpret what I had been observing in practice.

04 — Learning to explain

During the pandemic, running became a space for continuity and reflection. When activities resumed, I also entered the school system as a teacher. Teaching showed me that knowing something is not the same as making it understandable: complex ideas need clarity, listening and the right words.

05 — Seeing the bigger picture

I continued with a Master’s degree in Sport Management and Motor Activities to connect practice, education and organisation. It gave structure to an interest already present in my experience with the team: a sport project brings together people, goals, roles, resources and shared responsibilities.

06 — Back to the computer

Programming and web design brought me back to the curiosity of my early years. Building digital experiences still means breaking a problem into parts, understanding how they connect and creating something that works. This time, I returned to technology with everything I had learned along the way.

Different experiences. One common thread: understanding complex things, making them clear and building solutions that work.
