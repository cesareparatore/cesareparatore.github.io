Privacy notice

This notice explains what data may be processed when you browse the website or voluntarily contact me by email or WhatsApp.

Scope of this notice

Last updated: 12 November 2026. This notice reflects the current GitHub Pages hosting arrangement and must be updated after any hosting migration.

Data controller and contact

The data controller is Cesare Paratore. For any question about this notice or to exercise your rights under Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR), you can write to info@cesareparatore.it.

Which data may be processed

The website does not provide contact forms, private areas or registration systems. Contact data are received only if you voluntarily write to the published email address or start a conversation through WhatsApp. This may include the name or contact details you provide, the content of your message, the date of the communication and any attachments you choose to send.

Purposes and legal bases

Data received by email or WhatsApp are used to read and handle the request, reply, provide the requested information and, where applicable, take steps at your request before entering into a contract or manage a contractual relationship. Depending on the circumstances, the legal bases are the performance of pre-contractual steps or a contract (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR) and the legitimate interest in handling and replying to communications (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Sending health-related data

Please do not send medical certificates, medical reports, health information or other special-category data by email or WhatsApp unless strictly necessary to handle your request. If such data are sent voluntarily, their necessity and the applicable legal condition under Article 9 GDPR will be assessed. Unnecessary data will be deleted without undue delay. This warning does not automatically make the processing of health data lawful.

Service providers and recipients

GitHub Pages is used to publish the website. The email mailbox is provided through OVHcloud. If you choose WhatsApp, the communication is handled through WhatsApp and its infrastructure. These providers may process technical or communication data to deliver their services, according to their respective roles, terms, privacy notices and applicable agreements. Data may also be disclosed to authorised persons or to professionals/authorities where necessary to comply with legal obligations or protect rights. Data locations and any international transfers depend on the services and configurations actually used and must be checked in the relevant contractual documents.

Retention

Messages and any attachments are kept for as long as necessary to handle the request and any subsequent relationship. If the communication does not lead to a relationship or other documented need, the data will be deleted when no longer necessary. They may be kept longer where required to comply with legal obligations or establish, exercise or defend a legal claim. No numerical retention period is stated because one has not yet been defined and verified.

Browsing, cookies and tracking tools

The site does not use forms, accounts, analytics tools, advertising pixels, profiling systems, tracking scripts or iframes, and its code does not use document.cookie, localStorage or sessionStorage. No consent banner is displayed. The site is published on GitHub Pages, which logs and stores visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes, whether or not the visitor is signed in to GitHub. The site contains links that users may choose to open to external services, such as WhatsApp and their email application, which process data under their own notices. For more detail on providers, see “Service providers and recipients”.

Your rights

Where provided by the GDPR, you may request access to your data, rectification, erasure or restriction of processing, and object to processing; you may also exercise the right to data portability where applicable. Send your request to info@cesareparatore.it. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Italian Data Protection Authority (www.garanteprivacy.it). These rights are subject to the conditions and limits set by law.
